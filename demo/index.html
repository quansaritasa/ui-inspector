Dev notes (not part of the UI)
  • Layout: app bar, collapsible inputs, stats bar, then a 3-column workspace (tables 260px, raw text fluid, records 400px). Under 1180px the columns stack.
  • Highlights: one mark.hl per matched field value, colored by the table class (.t-*) through the --c variable. Solid underline for exact/normalized, dashed for fuzzy. Hover shows table, field and match type.
  • Match status chips: exact, normalized, fuzzy (with score), ambiguous (value appears more than once), no evidence (value not found, possible hallucination or inferred value).
  • Uncovered text: segments not covered by any highlight get class .unc, shown only when "Show uncovered text" is on. Coverage % = covered characters / total characters of the raw text.
  • Colors for many tables: assign a hue family per table group (for example parties = blues, items = greens, money = yellows) and vary lightness inside the group. Highlight alpha is separated into --hl-alpha so it can be tuned per theme.
  • Interactions (wired in this mock): click a highlight to select its field row, click a field row to scroll to and flash its highlight, table checkboxes toggle highlights.
  • Judge block: Phase 2. Per record: three scores, reason, evidence quote, QA verdict (Accept, Reject, comment). Export writes all verdicts to JSON.
  • Data in this mock is fictional.
